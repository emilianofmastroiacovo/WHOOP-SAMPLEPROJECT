Project 2

Creator fit report

Six creators evaluated across the full partnership spectrum. From a proven, signed ambassador to a clear mission mismatch being against WHOOP's own pillars: authenticity, data-backed storytelling, and athletic-journey framing. Professional athletes have been purposefully excluded to maintain a small scale project that does not take much time to review.

Ian Fonz (@fonzian)

Signed & working well

~588K Instagram · Confirmed WHOOP ambassador · Hybrid Athlete / Lifestyle creator · Austin, TX

Veteran, founder of the Raw Dawg Run Club, Gymshark athlete, and established hybrid athlete. Already runs an active WHOOP referral program and has done in-person branded content with WHOOP. His content is a textbook execution of the brand's core belief system: Highlighting the highs and lows of intense training while balancing mental health and listening to your body to maximize recovery and optimize performance.

He's not a performative influencer. He practices what he preaches. The consistent numbers and growth he gets across all of his social media pages plus his well-known personal brand of self-improvement is a perfect fit for WHOOP's image. He seems to be personable and relatable, despite being in the top 1% of physical performance - another crucial attribute that's incredibly valuable to WHOOP's message.

Verdict — Keep and expand. A textbook partnership WHOOP should point to when briefing new ambassadors while encouraging authenticity at the same time.

Luke Jagger (@lukejaggerfitness)

Great fit

~73.8K Instagram · 27.7K TikTok · Fitness / Hybrid Athlete creator · Tampa, FL

Gymshark athlete, and established hybrid athlete. His content is consistent: hard work and raw discipline. His reach is consistent across the board with people who are fitness-obsessed and prioritize physical fitness and recovery. Possesses a top-tier physique which demonstrates an extreme dedication and passion to what he does.

Less smiles and more grit. Shows personality but isn't far from a monotone creator. Generally makes great content that unintentionally motivates his audience. His numbers have been consistently growing over the past month, and it's clear that his personal mission aligns well with WHOOP's mission.

Verdict — Sign. A textbook hybrid athlete who embodies WHOOP's core values, and could provide value to one of WHOOP's target communities.

Emilio Fernandez (@emiliooofernandez)

Great fit

~5,000 followers · Endurance / triathlon creator · New York, NY

Content centers on endurance training balanced against mental health and knowing when too much is too much. High energy, personable, built on genuine discipline paired with exceptional recovery-awareness rather than a pure grind persona.

Mission alignment is close to WHOOP's own value proposition - strain versus recovery, listening to your body, and understanding your limits while pushing yourself. Cost and reach make this a low-risk signing with real upside, and the relatability of balancing endurance sport with a personal life is a more accessible entry point than elite-athlete content.

Verdict — Sign. Small platform, but overall great fit for WHOOP's mission and is a low cost to test. Our names also happen to be eerily similar.

Sofia Crnilovic (@sofiacrnilovic)

Potential fit

~381K Instagram · 1.5M TikTok · Lifestyle influencer · Virginia Beach, VA

Lifestyle, outfit, and makeup content or GRWMs, transitions, outfit reels. Audience skews heavily male, 18–24, across platforms. No visible training or recovery narrative as of today (9/1/2026).

The reach is a genuine gateway to a demographic that isn't already obsessed with fitness, but only if WHOOP is deliberately targeting lifestyle-first audiences with a "feel better throughout your day" mission over optimizing performance. There is a risk where a partnership reads as a paid placement since there's no prior similar contracts.

Verdict — Situational. Strong reach, but a content mismatch unless the strategy is audience expansion. I don't work for WHOOP (not yet at least) so I can't say for sure what the company is looking for.

Shane Stoffer (@togi)

Potential fit

Millions across TikTok, YouTube, Instagram · Gym/Weightlifting influencer · Bozeman, MT

High-energy bodybuilding influencer known for an extreme lifestyle content and a public history of drug use, including a 2024 overdose. He recently openly discussed sobriety and and an introduction to faith, alongside a financial collapse in 2026.

The sobriety arc is a genuinely compelling recovery narrative, but it's tangled with ongoing volatility that sits uncomfortably next to a science and discipline oriented brand. Authenticity is high but can be double-edged. The brand can't control what "authentic" looks like from him week to week, so that could be an issue or a blessing.

Verdict — Keep on radar, don't sign yet. Worth revisiting if the current trajectory holds, but his unpredictability is not be worth signing.

Tren Twins (Michael & Christian Gaiera)

Not a good fit

~2M Instagram · 1.7M TikTok · 1M+ YouTube combined · Gym/Weightlifting influencers · Texas, USA

Bodybuilding twins whose name directly references an anabolic steroid, and who've openly discussed steroid and testosterone use as part of their public persona. There's already a lot of public concern about their influence on underage audiences potentially experimenting with steroids.

WHOOP's brand is built on recovery science and self-knowledge through data and discipline which is the complete opposite of a brand built on blurring the lines around PED use. Reach doesn't offset a mission conflict this large.

Verdict — Hard pass. This partnership would work entirely against WHOOP's credibility on recovery and health.
